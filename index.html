01 / About

Systems & Aerospace

Hi! I’m Tanmay Tiwari, an Aerospace Engineering student at VIT Bhopal with a deep interest in building intelligent, data-driven systems. Originally from Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand, I’ve always been driven by the intersection of traditional engineering and modern computation.

Currently, I’m specializing in Generative AI and Data Science to explore how aerospace design can be enhanced through advanced computing. I have been consistently developing my Python expertise and am already applying those programming skills to solve practical engineering challenges.

For me, the most exciting work happens at the edge of disciplines—whether that’s using code to optimize a fuselage design or leveraging signal processing to uncover patterns that aren’t visible to the naked eye.

From my roots in Sitarganj to competing in hackathons, I approach every project with first-principles thinking and a commitment to innovation and excellence.

⚡ First Principles Thinking
I don't accept how things are — I question why they work the way they do. Every solution I build is grounded in foundational physics.
🚀 Systems Mindset
Complex problems rarely have isolated causes. I design with the entire system in view — from sensor input to mechanical output.
📊 Data-Driven Decisions
Intuition is a starting point, not an endpoint. I validate hypotheses with real data using Python, NumPy, and Pandas.
🤝 Open Collaboration
The best solutions come from multidisciplinary teams, like my work with Team PHOENIX during the Health Hackathon.
